Wall products in a room photo, at true size

Six public room photos, three products, fully automatic. Pretrained models find the walls and measure the room in metres, then the product is laid on each wall plane at its real repeat size. Furniture, doors, windows and skirting boards stay in front.

The biggest risk

Scale from one photo

There is no tape-measured ground truth for public photos, so a standard US interior door (2.03 m) is the yardstick. Depth Pro stays within ±5% wherever a door is visible. Depth Anything V2 reads 11–15% high and produced one impossible 24 m wall, so it is kept only as a cross-check: when the two models disagree, the product should ask the user for one reference.

The fallback is already built in. When a door is visible, the pipeline computes a correction factor (×1.046 and ×0.972 here) and can re-render at corrected scale: pick “50 cm grid, door-calibrated” in the viewer above. In the product this becomes one tap: “this is a door” or “this wall is 3.6 m wide”.

Pipeline

Existing models first, geometry after

Segment

Mask2Former Swin-L · ADE20K

Wall, floor, ceiling, door, window and every object in front of the wall.

Measure

Apple Depth Pro

Metric depth in metres plus the camera's focal length, from a single photo.

Separate light

Intrinsic decomposition v2.1

Splits surface colour from lighting, so dark brick or paint is not mistaken for shadow.

Fit planes

RANSAC, forced vertical

Floor plane gives “up”; each wall becomes a vertical plane; one wall split by depth noise is merged back.

Lay product

ray ∩ plane → metres

Every wall pixel gets a position in metres; the texture is read there, mip-filtered, relit with the room's light and composited.

Not solved yet

Known limitations

  • Mask edges are slightly soft at ceiling lines and around furniture. Next: refine with SAM 2, prompted by the wall mask.
  • Dark or mixed existing walls: the intrinsic model's albedo now evens out brick and plaster, but a slightly dark band remains under one window in the brick room.
  • Sockets and switches are papered over. A real install cuts around them: small detector or SAM 2 point prompts.
  • Scale is about ±5% at best from one photo and worse on some, so the one-tap reference is required, not optional.
  • Products here are generated textures with exact known size. Real catalogue images need a repeat size or DPI per SKU.
